Privacy Policy – Leevo

Effective Date: June 1, 2026
Developer: .zipcave (nino.zip94)
Contact: nino.zip94@gmail.com

Leevo ("the App", "we", "our") is a macOS app for recording, live-directing, editing, and exporting vertical creator videos. This Privacy Policy explains what information Leevo handles, how that information is used, how long it is kept, and how you can manage or delete it.

1. Information Leevo Handles

Leevo is designed as a local-first creator tool. The App may access or create the following information when you choose to use the related features:

  • Camera input when you select a camera as Source 1 or Source 2.
  • Microphone input when you choose an audio device for recording.
  • Screen, window, or app capture when you grant macOS Screen Recording permission.
  • Accessibility-related window information only for workflows that require macOS Accessibility permission.
  • Project data, including project names, timeline metadata, layout actions, source states, export settings, and watermark settings.
  • Media files, including raw recordings, autosaves, recovered recording chunks, exported videos, and custom watermark images.
  • Local monetization state, such as local export-credit counters and a Keychain-backed Pro entitlement cache.
  • Purchase and entitlement information, processed by RevenueCat and Paddle, such as product identifiers, purchase status, receipt or payment data, and an app user identifier needed to unlock Pro.

2. Information Leevo Does Not Collect

Leevo does not require a Leevo account, does not upload your recordings to Leevo servers, does not sell personal data, does not use third-party advertising SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not collect product analytics in the current implementation.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: used to preview and record a selected camera source.
  • Microphone: used to record selected audio input with your timeline.
  • Screen Recording: used to preview and record selected displays, apps, or windows.
  • Accessibility: may be requested for window-management workflows that need macOS accessibility access.

You can grant, deny, or revoke these permissions at any time in macOS System Settings. Some Leevo features may not work without the relevant permission.

4. How Information Is Used

Leevo uses local project and media information to:

  • Create, save, recover, and reopen Leevo projects.
  • Preview sources and live-directed layouts.
  • Record synchronized audio, screen, camera, and timeline metadata.
  • Recover interrupted recordings and autosaves where possible.
  • Render MP4 exports from raw media and timeline actions.
  • Apply Leevo watermark, custom watermark, or watermark-free export behavior.
  • Remember local export-credit state.
  • Display Pro purchase options, process purchases, activate Pro, restore access, prevent fraud, cache offline Pro access, and unlock or revoke Pro features.

5. Third-Party Services

Leevo does not share recordings, raw media, project files, timeline metadata, or custom watermark images with third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud storage services.

Leevo Pro is a one-time 19,99 EUR purchase for Leevo 1.x. It is not a subscription. Payments are processed by Paddle through RevenueCat purchase links. RevenueCat manages purchase status, entitlement validation, and activation.

Online activation is required after purchase. After successful activation, Leevo may allow offline Pro use for a limited validation window using a Keychain-backed entitlement cache. RevenueCat and Paddle may process purchase, payment, entitlement, device, app, and app user identifiers needed to provide Pro access, prevent fraud, process refunds, and meet legal obligations.

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Paddle Privacy Policy: paddle.com/legal/privacy
Local-first note: Leevo projects, raw media, autosaves, exports, and custom watermark files are stored on your Mac unless a future version clearly adds and discloses a cloud or sync feature.

6. Data Storage and Retention

  • Leevo keeps projects, raw media, autosaves, exports, and local settings until you delete them.
  • You can delete projects from Leevo's project library.
  • You can manage exported files and project packages from Finder.
  • You can remove local app data by deleting the App and its associated local files.
  • macOS permissions can be revoked in System Settings.
  • Leevo keeps offline Pro cache in Keychain for a limited validation window.
  • RevenueCat and Paddle retain purchase and entitlement records according to their own policies and legal obligations.

7. Children

Leevo is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it where appropriate.

8. Security

Leevo aims to collect and handle the minimum information needed for its recording, editing, recovery, and export features. Local files remain subject to the security of your Mac, your user account, and your storage choices. No system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Leevo's features, data handling, purchases, or services change. Updates will appear on this page and take effect when posted.
